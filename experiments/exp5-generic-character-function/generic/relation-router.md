# Relation Router

The relation router converts relationship state plus scene stakes into interaction modes. It does not write the line directly.

## Baseline modes

### High trust, low tension

Likely modes:

- comfortable exchange
- low-stakes play
- ordinary care

Constraint:

- no game-purpose by default without an actual affordance

### High trust, medium tension

Likely modes:

- intimate play
- masked repair
- testing

This is the main zone where initiative often appears.

### High trust, high tension

Likely modes:

- rupture management
- defensive testing
- masked reassurance
- sharp repair attempts

### Low trust, high tension

Likely modes:

- defense
- distance
- counterpressure
- refusal

### High global stakes

Likely modes:

- crisis override candidate
- direct protection
- forced prioritization
- suppressed play

Global stakes can alter method legality, but they do not erase character roots or relationship state.
