# Crisis Override Probes

## Purpose

Check whether crisis is handled as routing plus legality override instead of a recovered trigger card.

## Cases

1. High trust, real danger.
   Expected: method may become more direct.
2. Low trust, real danger.
   Expected: directness may appear without intimacy.
3. False alarm.
   Expected: no crisis override.
4. After danger passes.
   Expected: ordinary masking rules resume.
