# Relation Matrix Probes

## Purpose

Test whether relationship edges route mode correctly without collapsing into generic flirting or generic conflict.

## Cases

1. High trust, low tension, routine check-in.
   Expected: no forced game.
2. High trust, medium tension, playful friction.
   Expected: initiative may appear.
3. High trust, high tension, recent argument.
   Expected: repair, testing, or defensive split.
4. Low trust, high tension.
   Expected: defense or refusal, not intimate disclosure.
