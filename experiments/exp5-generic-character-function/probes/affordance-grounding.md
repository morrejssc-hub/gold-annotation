# Affordance Grounding Probes

## Purpose

Prevent the old false-positive pattern where a normal question gets inflated into a power contest.

## Cases

1. Routine inquiry with no leverage.
   Expected: no contest by default.
2. Concern packaged with control.
   Expected: contest becomes possible.
3. Clear overreach.
   Expected: initiative or counterpressure is allowed.
4. Care signal without pressure.
   Expected: response may stay ordinary or warm.
